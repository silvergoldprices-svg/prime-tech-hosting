# About PrimeTech Hosting

PrimeTech Hosting is an independent website that publishes reviews, comparisons and guides about web hosting.

## What we do

We help people choose a hosting provider by explaining plans, features and trade-offs in plain English. Where we have not tested a provider ourselves, we say so clearly and base the page on the provider's own documentation.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Read the full [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
