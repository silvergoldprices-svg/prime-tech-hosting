# Our Review Methodology

## Two kinds of pages

Every provider page on this site is labelled in one of two ways:

- **Hands-on review:** written after we signed up for and used the service. We describe only what we actually saw.
- **Overview based on provider documentation:** written from the provider's public website and documentation. We have not tested the service, and the page says so.

## Prices and specifications

Prices, plan limits and features are kept in one data file and shown on every page that mentions them. Each provider shows a "Last verified" date. Hosting prices change often and usually rise at renewal, so always confirm on the provider's site before you buy.

When we cannot confirm a figure, we say "Check the provider's site for current pricing" instead of guessing.

## What we look at

- Plan types and who each one suits
- Introductory price versus renewal price
- Money-back guarantee terms
- Included features such as SSL, backups and a free domain
- Support options
- Test results (speed, uptime) only when we ran the test ourselves and can show the method

## What we do not do

We do not invent ratings, testimonials, test results or publication dates. Affiliate relationships do not buy a better review.
