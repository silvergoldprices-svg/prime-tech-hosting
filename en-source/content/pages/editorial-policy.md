# Editorial Policy

## Accuracy

We base pages on official provider information or on our own hands-on use, and we label which one. Facts that we cannot verify are left out or marked clearly.

## Independence

Providers cannot pay for placement in our rankings or for changes to our conclusions.

## Updates

Pages show the date they were published and the date they were last updated. We only change the "updated" date when the content really changed.

## Corrections

If you spot an error, tell us through the [Contact page](/contact/). We correct confirmed mistakes and update the page.
