# Bluehost Student Discount: Is There a Special Offer?

Yes, Bluehost points to a student offer. The Programs section of Bluehost's site includes a "Student Discount" link, and that link goes to a Bluehost page on Student Beans, a student verification service. We checked this on November 3, 2026.

## What we could confirm

- Bluehost lists a student discount link on its own site.
- The offer is delivered through Student Beans, so you would need to verify that you are a student.

## What we could not confirm

We could not see the discount amount, the eligibility rules or the plans it covers, because those details sit on the Student Beans page. We therefore do not state a percentage here. We also cannot say whether it can be combined with Bluehost's regular promotional pricing. Bluehost says some of its current offers cannot be combined with other offers, so check the terms.

## How to check the offer

1. Open the Student Discount link in the Programs section at the bottom of the [Bluehost site](host:bluehost).
2. Follow the verification steps on Student Beans.
3. Compare the final price at checkout with the standard price in our [Bluehost pricing guide](/bluehost/bluehost-pricing/).

Remember that Bluehost renewals use regular prices, as shown in our [renewal cost guide](/bluehost/bluehost-renewal-cost/).
