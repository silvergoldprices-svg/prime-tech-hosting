# Bluehost Free Domain: How It Works and What It Costs to Renew

Bluehost includes one free domain for the first year with a hosting plan, but there are rules. This guide summarizes Bluehost's own free domain policy, checked on November 3, 2026.

## Who gets the free domain

You get one free domain for one year when you buy a web hosting plan on a 12-month or 36-month term. You must redeem it within 90 days of the purchase date.

## Which domains qualify

The free domain applies to a domain transfer into Bluehost or to a new domain purchase in one of these extensions: .biz, .info, .com, .net, .org, .uk, .co.uk, .us, .eu, .de, .nl, .co, .space, .website, .blog, .online, .site, .store and .tech. Premium domain names are excluded. Bluehost also notes a value limit of $17.99 for the first year, so a domain that costs more needs a payment at checkout.

## What it costs after year one

After the first year, the domain renews automatically at the then-current rate shown in your Renewal Center, until you cancel. We do not list a renewal price here because it depends on the extension. Bluehost keeps a domain price list in its help center at [bluehost.com/help/article/domain-name-pricing](https://www.bluehost.com/help/article/domain-name-pricing).

## Rules that can cost you money

- **Refunds:** if you cancel hosting within 30 days and received a free domain, Bluehost deducts a non-refundable domain fee from your refund.
- **Cancelling in year one:** domains cancelled within the first year are charged a non-refundable fee based on the standard domain cost.
- **Transfers:** a newly registered domain cannot be moved to another registrar during its first 60 days.
- **Expired domains:** Bluehost's FAQ describes a 45-day renewal grace period, then a 30-day redemption period with a $99.95 reactivation fee plus the renewal fee. It says the figures were accurate as of September 10, 2025, so check the current rules.

## Should you rely on the free domain?

If you want to keep your domain long term, plan for its renewal cost and check the price before you accept it. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Bluehost domain really free?

It is free for the first year on 12 or 36 month hosting terms, within Bluehost's conditions. It then renews at the regular rate.

### Do I have to claim it right away?

You must redeem it within 90 days of buying your hosting plan.

### What happens to the domain if I cancel hosting?

If you cancel within 30 days, Bluehost deducts a domain fee from your refund. Domains cancelled in the first year are also charged a non-refundable fee.
