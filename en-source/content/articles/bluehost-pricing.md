# Bluehost Pricing 2026: Plans, Costs, and Renewal Rates

Bluehost shows a low promotional price, then a higher price when the first term ends. This guide lays out the current shared hosting prices from Bluehost's own pricing page, checked on November 3, 2026, so you can see both numbers.

## Current shared hosting prices

These are the prices Bluehost shows for the 36-month term. A toggle on the pricing page also offers a 12-month term, and the price changes with the term, so check the one you plan to buy.

| Plan | Promo price (36 months) | Bluehost's saving label | Renews at (36 months) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $3.99/mo | Save 60% | $9.99/mo |
| Business | $6.99/mo | Save 50% | $13.99/mo |
| eCommerce Essentials | $14.99/mo | Save 32% | $21.99/mo |

The "save" labels compare the promotional price with the renewal price, which we confirmed with simple arithmetic. For example, $3.99 is 60% below $9.99.

## What the price includes

Bluehost lists free SSL, a free domain for the first year on 12 or 36 month terms, weekly website backups, malware scanning and a free WordPress migration tool on all three plans. Starter allows 10 websites and 10 GB NVMe storage, Business allows 50 websites and 50 GB, and eCommerce Essentials allows 100 websites and 100 GB. Read more in our [Bluehost review](/reviews/bluehost/).

## Fees and extras to check

- **Promo prices are for the first term only.** Bluehost says special offers are valid for the initial term and not for renewals.
- **Free domain renews at the regular rate.** After the first year it renews at the then-current rate shown in your Renewal Center.
- **Pro Email trial.** Bluehost offers a one-month free trial of Professional Email, which renews at $2.99 per month unless you cancel before the first billing cycle ends.
- **Taxes.** The pricing page notes that VAT or GST is not included in the displayed price.

Bluehost also states that there are no hidden fees on its hosting plans, and that optional add-ons such as domain privacy or email can change your total.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest Bluehost plan?

On Bluehost's pricing page, Starter is the cheapest shared plan at $3.99 per month on a 36-month term as of November 3, 2026.

### Does the promotional price last after the first term?

No. Bluehost says promotional prices apply to the initial term only. After that, the plan renews at the regular rate shown in your control panel.

### Are there hidden fees?

Bluehost states that there are no hidden fees and that the hosting cost you see at checkout is what you pay. Optional add-ons and taxes can change the total.
