# Bluehost 60% Off Deal: How to Get the Best Price

Bluehost's pricing page shows a "Save 60%" label on its Starter plan. This guide explains what that number means, based on the page as we saw it on November 3, 2026.

## Where the 60% comes from

The label appears on the Starter plan on the 36-month term. Bluehost shows $3.99 per month, renewing at $9.99 per month. The saving is measured against that renewal price: $3.99 is 60% less than $9.99. The other shared plans show smaller savings.

| Plan | Promo price (36 months) | Label | Renews at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $3.99/mo | Save 60% | $9.99/mo |
| Business | $6.99/mo | Save 50% | $13.99/mo |
| eCommerce Essentials | $14.99/mo | Save 32% | $21.99/mo |

## How to get that price

On the pricing page, pick the 36-month term and choose Starter. The page we viewed did not ask for a coupon code. If a coupon field or a different price appears at checkout, trust what the checkout shows.

## What the 60% does not mean

- It is not a discount on your renewal. Bluehost says promotional prices are valid for the initial term only.
- It is not available on every term. The price changes if you choose 12 months.
- It does not include taxes, which the pricing page says are not included.

## Is it the best price?

It is the lowest monthly price on the page, but you commit for 36 months, and the renewal price is about two and a half times higher.
