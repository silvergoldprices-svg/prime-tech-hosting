## What Hostinger sells

Hostinger is a Lithuania-based provider that sells shared web hosting, cloud hosting, VPS hosting and email hosting. This page is an overview based on Hostinger's published pricing information. We have not tested the service ourselves, so it contains no speed or uptime results.

## Shared and cloud plans

The figures below come from Hostinger's own pricing information and were checked on November 3, 2026. Prices shown are the promotional rate on a 48-month term.

| Plan | Websites | Storage | Backups | Promo price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Premium | 3 | 20 GB SSD | Weekly | $2.49/mo |
| Unlimited | Unlimited | 50 GB NVMe | Daily | $3.49/mo |
| Cloud Startup | Check the provider's site | 100 GB NVMe, 4 CPU cores, 4 GB RAM | Check the provider's site | $7.99/mo |

Plan names and limits change over time, so confirm them on the [Hostinger pricing page](host:hostinger) before you buy.

## Intro price versus renewal price

The low headline prices need a long upfront commitment. Hostinger lists the Unlimited plan at $18.99 per month on a rolling monthly basis, compared with $3.49 on the 48-month term. Hostinger also shows a renewal line at checkout (the renewal price is higher than the promotional one), so read it before paying. Our [review methodology](/review-methodology/) explains why we always show both prices.

## VPS hosting

Hostinger's self-managed VPS hosting starts at $6.49 per month on a 24-month term, with four plans going up to $25.99 per month. Self-managed means you handle server setup and maintenance yourself.

## Included features

- Unlimited free SSL on its web hosting plans
- A free domain on subscriptions of 12 months or longer
- On the Unlimited plan: CDN and AI tools, according to Hostinger

## Who this may suit

Based on the published plans, Hostinger's lowest prices go to people who can pay for several years upfront. If you prefer to pay monthly or yearly, compare the real cost on the [hosting comparison table](/tools/compare/) and the other providers we cover: [Bluehost](/bluehost/) and [HostGator](/hostgator/).
