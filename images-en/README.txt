English article images
======================
Your own picture for an English article goes here:  images-en/<article-name>/<article-name>-NN.jpg
  - NN = 01 is the top (hero) image, 02, 03 ... are the pictures between paragraphs.
  - The exact file name is printed on every placeholder picture shown on the live page.
  - JPG only (lower-case .jpg). Wider than 1600 px is shrunk automatically.
Example: images-en/bluehost-coupon-not-working/bluehost-coupon-not-working-02.jpg
